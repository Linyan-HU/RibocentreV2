# Ribozyme families

Browse the small and large ribozymes catalogued in Ribocentre. Each record links to its family page, discovery paper and available Rfam entries. Discovery years refer to the experimental identification of catalytic activity.

Small nucleolytic ribozymes typically span about 50–150 nucleotides and catalyse site-specific cleavage. Some also catalyse the reverse ligation reaction. The large ribozymes include group I and group II introns, RNase P, the ribosome and the spliceosome.
